# Career Story

## Who I Am

I am a Software Engineer with nearly four years of experience working on enterprise SaaS applications.

My expertise lies in backend engineering, production troubleshooting, SQL, API integrations, Python automation, and building scalable technical solutions that improve business efficiency.

Throughout my career, I have worked closely with engineering teams, product managers, and enterprise customers across the US and EMEA regions, solving production issues and improving software reliability.

## My Journey

I started my professional career as a Software Educator, where I developed strong communication skills and learned how to explain technical concepts clearly.

I then joined Anthology, where I spent almost four years supporting enterprise SaaS platforms.

Although my designation was Senior Product Analyst, my day-to-day work involved solving engineering problems rather than performing traditional business analysis.

I regularly worked with:

- SQL
- Python
- APIs
- ETL
- Performance Optimization
- Root Cause Analysis
- Production Debugging

These experiences naturally led me toward Backend Software Engineering.
